# Ads playbook — ISS GST Billing (India)

Use after pricing + payments are live. Goal: cheap enquiries that convert to Growth annual (₹3,499 + GST).

## Positioning

- Hook: “GST billing from ₹167/mo (yearly) — cheaper than Vyapar & myBillBook”
- Proof: branded PDFs, WhatsApp share, multi-user roles, 14-day trial
- CTA: Request demo / Start Growth plan → landing `#pricing` + enquiry popup
- Landing: https://billing.indiansoftwareservices.com/

## Google Search (highest intent)

**Budget start:** ₹400–600/day  
**Campaign type:** Search, India, Hindi+English  
**Keywords (exact / phrase):**
- GST billing software
- GST invoice software
- billing software for shop
- GST bill software India
- [city] GST billing software (Delhi, Mumbai, Surat, Ahmedabad, Jaipur)

**Ad copy sketch:**
- H1: GST Billing from ₹167/mo
- H2: Multi-user · WhatsApp share · Branded PDF
- Desc: 14-day trial. Hospitals, schools, retail. Talk to Platform Admin today.

**UTM:** `?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=gst_billing`

## Meta (Facebook / Instagram)

**Budget start:** ₹200–300/day  
**Audience:** India, 25–50, interests: small business, retail, Kirana, GST, accounting  
**Creative:** screenshot of branded invoice + price overlay “₹3,499/year”  
**CTA:** Learn more → landing pricing  
**Retarget:** site visitors 7 days who didn’t enquire

## Offer

- 14-day Growth trial
- Launch promo (optional): first year Growth ₹2,999 if you want volume

## Tracking

- Enquiry form already stores messages — ask “Interested in Growth” from pricing CTA
- Weekly: cost per enquiry, paid conversions, plan mix in Platform → Plans MRR strip

## Do / Don’t

- Do: send every lead a WhatsApp within 1 hour with Client ID + trial
- Don’t: bid on “Tally free download” or brand-only competitor terms until you have reviews
